## Lesson Plan (32 Lessons)

### Lesson 1: Introduction — The AHIMA Domain Framework
- What AHIMA is, its role in HIM profession
- The 8 HIIM domains and how they interconnect
- RHIT vs RHIA certification — what each tests
- How the domains map to your career
- Exam structure: 150 questions, 5 scored domains, weights

### UNIT 1: Domain I — Data Structure, Content & Information Governance (19-25%)

### Lesson 2: The Health Record — Content, Structure & Legal Requirements
- Legal health record (LHR) vs Designated Record Set (DRS)
- Medical record components by care setting (acute, ambulatory, LTC, home health)
- Documentation requirements: CMS CoPs, TJC, state law
- Authentication, authorship, timeliness (H&P within 24 hrs, discharge summary 30 days)
- Amendments vs corrections vs addenda vs late entries

### Lesson 3: Data Sets, Registries & Secondary Data Sources
- ALL major data sets: UHDDS (inpatient), UACDS (ambulatory), MDS 3.0 (SNF), OASIS-E (home health), IRF-PAI (rehab), DEEDS (ED), HEDIS (managed care)
- Cancer registries (NAACCR, AJCC staging, accession numbers)
- Trauma registries (NTDB), immunization registries (IIS), birth defect registries
- Indices: MPI, disease index, procedure index, physician index
- Data dictionaries and metadata
- Vital statistics reporting

### Lesson 4: Health Record Formats, EHR Documentation & Record Management
- Source-oriented, problem-oriented (POMR/SOAP), integrated, hybrid formats
- Quantitative analysis (completeness) vs qualitative analysis (consistency)
- EHR documentation: structured vs unstructured, templates, copy-forward risks
- Record retention: state minimums, AHIMA recommends 10 yrs adult
- Destruction methods: shredding, degaussing, overwriting, destruction certificates

### Lesson 5: Master Patient Index & Information Governance
- MPI purpose, data elements, duplicate management, EMPI
- AHIMA IG Principles: accountability, transparency, integrity, protection, compliance, availability, retention, disposition
- Data stewardship roles
- AHIMA Data Quality Model (10 characteristics — KNOW ALL 10)
- Data integrity, data mapping, metadata management

### Lesson 6: Clinical Vocabularies, Classifications & Terminology Systems
- Classification vs nomenclature vs terminology (definitions matter)
- ICD-10-CM (NCHS), ICD-10-PCS (CMS), CPT (AMA), HCPCS Level II (CMS)
- SNOMED CT (NLM), LOINC (lab), RxNorm (meds), CDT (dental), DSM-5 (mental health)
- NDC (drug ID, FDA), ICD-O-3 (oncology)
- UMLS (Metathesaurus, Semantic Network, SPECIALIST Lexicon)
- GEMs (General Equivalence Mappings), mapping between systems

### UNIT 2: Domain II — Information Protection: Privacy & Security (14-18%)

### Lesson 7: HIPAA Privacy Rule — Everything You Need to Know
- PHI definition, 18 HIPAA identifiers (LIST ALL 18)
- Covered entities (3 types), business associates, BAA requirements
- Patient rights: access (30 days), amendment (60 days), accounting of disclosures (6 yr), restrict, confidential communications
- TPO definitions, minimum necessary standard (and its exceptions)
- Authorization requirements (6 core + 3 statements)
- Personal representatives, minors, deceased (50 yr), psychotherapy notes
- 42 CFR Part 2 (substance abuse — more restrictive than HIPAA)

### Lesson 8: HIPAA Security Rule & Breach Notification
- Security Rule scope (ePHI only)
- Administrative safeguards (security officer, training, contingency plan, BAA)
- Physical safeguards (facility access, workstation security, device/media controls)
- Technical safeguards (access controls, audit controls, integrity, transmission security)
- Required vs Addressable (addressable ≠ optional)
- Breach definition, 4-factor risk assessment
- Notification timelines (60 days individuals, 60 days HHS if 500+)
- HITECH penalty tiers (4 tiers), criminal penalties (DOJ)

### Lesson 9: Release of Information & Disclosure Management
- ROI workflow: receive → validate → verify identity → scope → process → track → invoice
- Valid authorization (6 core + 3 statements)
- Subpoena vs court order (satisfactory assurance requirements)
- Permitted disclosures WITHOUT authorization (public health, law enforcement, research, etc.)
- Research disclosures: waiver criteria, preparatory to research, decedent research
- Fees: HIPAA right of access (reasonable cost-based), state maximums

### Lesson 10: Access Management & Emerging Privacy Challenges
- Role-based access control (RBAC), break-the-glass procedures
- Audit trails and monitoring
- Patient portals and HIPAA right of access
- Information blocking (21st Century Cures Act)
- State privacy laws (more restrictive than HIPAA in some states)
- Emerging: telehealth privacy, mobile health data, social media policies

### UNIT 3: Domain III — Informatics, Analytics & Data Use (12-18%)

### Lesson 11: Health Information Systems & EHR
- EHR vs EMR vs PHR distinctions
- EHR core functions: CPOE, CDS, e-prescribing, clinical documentation, patient portal, HIE
- System lifecycle: strategic planning → needs assessment → RFP → vendor selection → implementation → go-live → optimization
- Interoperability levels: foundational, structural, semantic, organizational
- Standards: HL7 v2, HL7 FHIR, CDA/C-CDA, X12 EDI (837, 835, 270/271, 276/277, 278)
- 21st Century Cures Act, information blocking, TEFCA

### Lesson 12: Healthcare Statistics — Rates, Formulas & Calculations
- Measures of central tendency: mean, median, mode
- Mortality rates: gross death rate, net death rate, newborn/fetal/maternal
- Autopsy rates: gross, net, hospital-adjusted
- Infection rates: nosocomial, community-acquired
- Bed statistics: occupancy rate, bed turnover, ADC, ALOS
- ALL FORMULAS with worked examples
- Descriptive vs inferential statistics, correlation, reliability vs validity

### Lesson 13: Quality & Performance Improvement
- IOM "To Err Is Human" and "Crossing the Quality Chasm" (STEEEP)
- Donabedian model: Structure → Process → Outcome
- PI tools: PDCA/PDSA, RCA (after sentinel event), FMEA (proactive, before event)
- Fishbone/Ishikawa, Pareto chart (80/20), run charts, control charts
- CMS quality programs: Hospital VBP, HRRP, HAC Reduction
- TJC National Patient Safety Goals, core measures, eCQMs, HCAHPS
- CDI program metrics

### Lesson 14: Research Methods & Clinical Decision Support
- Study types: RCT, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional
- Quantitative vs qualitative research
- Sampling methods, p-value, Type I vs Type II error, confidence intervals
- IRB levels: exempt, expedited, full review
- Belmont Report: respect for persons, beneficence, justice
- Evidence hierarchy
- CDS: knowledge-based vs non-knowledge-based, five rights of CDS

### UNIT 4: Domain IV — Revenue Cycle Management (19-25%) — LARGEST SECTION

### Lesson 15: ICD-10-CM Coding — Guidelines & High-Yield Chapters
- Official Coding Guidelines structure and navigation
- Code to highest specificity, signs/symptoms coding, combination codes
- Laterality, 7th characters (A/D/S for injuries)
- Excludes1 (NEVER together) vs Excludes2 (CAN if documented)
- HIGH-YIELD: Diabetes (E08-E13), Hypertension (I10-I16), MI (STEMI vs NSTEMI), Sepsis (A40-A41), Pregnancy, Neoplasms, Injuries

### Lesson 16: ICD-10-PCS — Structure, Root Operations & Approaches
- 7-character structure (section, body system, root operation, body part, approach, device, qualifier)
- 31 root operations in Medical & Surgical section
- MOST TESTED: Excision vs Resection, Repair vs Supplement, Bypass, Drainage, Replacement vs Supplement
- All 7 approaches: Open, Percutaneous, Perc Endo, Via Natural, Via Natural Endo, Via Natural w/ Perc Endo Assist, External
- Code-building exercises

### Lesson 17: CPT & HCPCS Coding
- CPT 6 sections (E/M, Anesthesia, Surgery, Radiology, Path/Lab, Medicine)
- E/M 2021 guidelines: MDM vs time-based
- MDM table: 4 levels × 3 elements
- Key modifiers: -25, -26, -TC, -59, -50, -51, -52, -57, -76/-77
- Surgical global period (0/10/90 days)
- HCPCS Level II structure
- NCCI edits: Column 1/2, MUE, modifier indicators

### Lesson 18: Reimbursement Systems & Payment Methodologies
- Fee-for-service vs prospective vs value-based
- ALL PPS systems table: IPPS/MS-DRG, OPPS/APC, MPFS/RVU, SNF PPS/PDPM, HH PPS/PDGM, IRF PPS/CMG, LTCH PPS/MS-LTC-DRG, Hospice/4 levels
- Chargemaster/CDM management
- Claim forms: UB-04 (institutional), CMS-1500 (professional)
- EOB vs ERA/RA, coordination of benefits, MSP rules

### Lesson 19: Revenue Cycle — Front End to Back End
- Complete revenue cycle: scheduling → pre-reg → insurance verification → registration → charge capture → coding → claim scrubbing → submission (837) → remittance (835) → payment posting → denial management → appeals → collections
- Key metrics: days in A/R (<40), clean claim rate (>95%), denial rate (<5%), DNFB, DNFC
- Case mix index (CMI) calculation
- Medicare appeals (5 levels)

### Lesson 20: Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI) & Coding Compliance
- CDI programs: concurrent review, physician queries
- Compliant query construction (non-leading)
- CC/MCC capture rates, CMI impact
- Coding accuracy audits
- Upcoding, unbundling, DRG creep — fraud indicators
- OIG Work Plan coding targets

### Lesson 21: Value-Based Care & Modern Reimbursement
- MACRA/QPP: MIPS (4 categories, weights, payment adjustments) vs APMs
- ACO models: MSSP, one-sided vs two-sided risk
- Bundled payments: BPCI Advanced
- Risk adjustment: HCC coding, RAF scores
- Star ratings, pay-for-performance, public reporting

### UNIT 5: Domain V — Health Law & Compliance (13-17%)

### Lesson 22: Compliance Programs & Regulatory Framework
- OIG 7 elements of effective compliance program
- Compliance officer role and reporting structure
- Corporate Integrity Agreements (CIAs)
- Accreditation: TJC (deemed status), DNV GL, CARF, NCQA, AAAHC
- CMS CoPs/CfCs
- State licensure vs certification vs accreditation

### Lesson 23: Fraud, Abuse & Healthcare Law
- False Claims Act: civil, treble damages, qui tam/whistleblower
- Anti-Kickback Statute: criminal, "one purpose" test, safe harbors
- Stark Law: civil, physician self-referral, DHS list (12 services), exceptions
- EMTALA: MSE + stabilize/transfer
- OIG exclusion list (LEIE), ZPICs/UPICs
- Coding fraud indicators: upcoding, unbundling, phantom billing, duplicate billing

### Lesson 24: Ethics, Legal Proceedings & Health Information Law
- AHIMA Code of Ethics (11 principles)
- AHIMA Standards of Ethical Coding (12 standards)
- Legal proceedings: subpoena, court order, discovery, e-discovery
- Malpractice, negligence, informed consent
- Advance directives, POLST
- Record amendment rights and processes

### UNIT 6: Domain VI — Organizational Management & Leadership (9-12%)

### Lesson 25: HIM Department Management & Operations
- HIM department functions: coding, ROI, transcription, CDI, cancer registry, data quality
- Workflow analysis and redesign
- Staffing models, productivity standards
- Budget management: operating vs capital, FTE calculations
- Outsourcing considerations
- Policies and procedures development

### Lesson 26: Leadership, Human Resources & Change Management
- Leadership theories: transformational, servant, situational
- HR functions: recruitment, orientation, training, performance evaluation
- Disciplinary process, termination
- Change management models (Kotter's 8 steps, Lewin's freeze model)
- Team dynamics, conflict resolution
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion in healthcare

### Lesson 27: Project Management & Performance Improvement Leadership
- Project management fundamentals: scope, schedule, budget, risk
- PMBOK knowledge areas
- Agile vs waterfall
- PDCA/PDSA applied to HIM
- Benchmarking, best practices
- Strategic planning and SWOT analysis
- Consumer engagement and patient advocacy

### UNIT 7: Domain VII — Clinical Foundations (Not Standalone Exam Domain)

### Lesson 28: Anatomy, Physiology & Medical Terminology Foundations
- Body systems overview relevant to coding and HIM
- Medical terminology: prefixes, roots, suffixes
- Anatomical planes, directional terms, body cavities
- Key body systems: cardiovascular, respiratory, musculoskeletal, nervous, digestive, genitourinary, integumentary, endocrine
- How clinical knowledge connects to coding accuracy

### Lesson 29: Pathophysiology, Pharmacology & Diagnostic Procedures
- Common disease processes by body system
- Pharmacology basics: drug classifications, routes of administration
- Diagnostic procedures: labs, imaging, functional tests
- How diagnoses are confirmed and documented
- Clinical documentation patterns that drive coding

### UNIT 8: Domain VIII — Evolving Topics (Not Standalone Exam Domain)

### Lesson 30: Emerging Technologies & HIM Innovation
- Artificial Intelligence in healthcare and HIM
- Natural Language Processing for clinical documentation
- Blockchain for health data integrity
- Telehealth/telemedicine regulations and documentation
- FHIR and modern interoperability
- Cloud computing and health data

### Lesson 31: Risk Adjustment, HCCs & Future of Reimbursement
- Hierarchical Condition Categories (HCC) deep dive
- Risk Adjustment Factor (RAF) scores
- Medicare Advantage risk adjustment
- Prospective vs concurrent vs retrospective review
- Social determinants of health (SDOH) coding
- Future of value-based care

### Lesson 32: Capstone — Mastering the AHIMA Framework
- All 8 domains interconnected
- Cross-domain scenario analysis
- RHIT exam strategy and domain weighting review
- Career pathways in HIM
- Professional development and continuing education
- AHIMA credential maintenance requirements
