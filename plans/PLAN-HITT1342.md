## Lesson Plan (22 Lessons)

### Unit 1: CPT Foundations (L1-5)
1. **"Introduction to Ambulatory/Outpatient Coding"** — Ambulatory care settings, outpatient vs inpatient coding differences, key code sets (CPT, HCPCS II, ICD-10-CM for outpatient), CMS-1500 vs UB-04, physician-based vs facility-based coding, outpatient coding guidelines (no "rule-out" diagnoses — code signs/symptoms instead)
2. **"CPT Structure & Organization"** — AMA ownership, CPT Category I (6 sections), Category II (performance measures), Category III (emerging technology), code format (5 digits), semicolon convention, standalone vs indented codes, add-on codes (+), modifier-51 exempt, CPT index, Appendices A-P
3. **"CPT Guidelines, Conventions & Modifiers Part 1"** — CPT conventions: parenthetical notes, cross-references, separate procedure, unlisted codes, special reports. Common modifiers: -25 (significant/separately identifiable E/M), -26 (professional component), -TC (technical component), -59 (distinct procedural service), -76/-77 (repeat procedures)
4. **"CPT Modifiers Part 2 & NCCI Edits"** — Modifiers -50 (bilateral), -LT/-RT, -51 (multiple procedures), -52 (reduced services), -53 (discontinued), -22 (increased procedural services), -24/-25/-57 (E/M modifiers). NCCI (National Correct Coding Initiative) edits: Column 1/Column 2, mutually exclusive, modifier indicators (0, 1, 9), MUE (Medically Unlikely Edits)
5. **"HCPCS Level II Coding"** — CMS maintenance, code structure (letter + 4 digits), sections A-V, temporary codes (C, G, K, Q, S, T), DME coding (E codes), drug coding (J codes), ambulance (A0 codes), HCPCS modifiers (anatomical: FA-TA fingers/toes, LC-RC coronary arteries, E1-E4 eyelids), ABN (Advance Beneficiary Notice) and GA/GX/GY/GZ modifiers

### Unit 2: Evaluation & Management (L6-10)
6. **"E/M Coding Fundamentals — 2021 Framework"** — 2021 E/M guidelines revolution (eliminated 1995/1997 history/exam requirements for office visits), now based on MDM OR total time, categories of E/M codes, place of service impact, new vs established patient (3-year rule)
7. **"Medical Decision Making (MDM) — The Table"** — MDM table: 4 levels (straightforward, low, moderate, high), 3 elements (number/complexity of problems, data reviewed/analyzed/ordered, risk of complications/morbidity/mortality), must meet or exceed 2 of 3. Problem types, data element counting, risk table with examples
8. **"Time-Based E/M Coding"** — Total time on date of encounter (2021), what counts (preparing, obtaining/reviewing records, ordering, communicating, care coordination, documenting), time ranges per level, prolonged services (99417), split/shared visits (2023 rules), teaching physician rules
9. **"Other E/M Categories"** — Hospital inpatient/observation (99221-99236), consultations (99241-99255, Medicare doesn't pay), ED visits (99281-99285, still use 1995/1997 guidelines), critical care (99291-99292, time-based), nursing facility, domiciliary, home services, care management (CCM 99490, TCM 99495-99496)
10. **"E/M Auditing & Compliance"** — Auditing E/M charts, documentation requirements, cloning concerns, copy-forward risks, medical necessity, ABN, incident-to billing rules, split/shared compliance, locum tenens, telehealth E/M (place of service 02/10), common E/M errors

### Unit 3: CPT Procedural Coding (L11-17)
11. **"Anesthesia Coding (00100-01999)"** — Anesthesia formula: Base units + Time units + Modifying factors × Conversion factor, time reporting (per 15 min), physical status modifiers (P1-P6), qualifying circumstances codes (99100-99140), monitored anesthesia care (MAC), moderate/conscious sedation, CRNA vs anesthesiologist billing
12. **"Surgery Section Overview & Integumentary (10000-19999)"** — Surgical package (global period: 0, 10, 90 days), what's included (pre-op day of, intra-op, typical post-op), separate procedures, staged procedures, wound repair (simple/intermediate/complex, length-based), skin lesion excision (diameter + margins), Mohs, breast procedures, burns
13. **"Musculoskeletal & Respiratory Systems (20000-32999)"** — Fracture treatment (closed/open, manipulation, external/internal fixation), arthroscopy, spinal procedures, joint replacement. Respiratory: endoscopy, bronchoscopy, thoracentesis, chest tube, lung procedures — scope vs open approach coding
14. **"Cardiovascular & Hemic/Lymphatic (33000-38999)"** — Cardiac catheterization, pacemaker/ICD (leads + generator), CABG, vascular access, dialysis access, endovascular procedures (stents, atherectomy), interventional radiology crossover. Hemic: bone marrow biopsy, splenectomy, lymph node procedures
15. **"Digestive & Urinary Systems (40000-53899)"** — Endoscopy coding (base procedure, multiple endoscopy rule), EGD, colonoscopy (screening vs diagnostic vs therapeutic), appendectomy, hernia repair. Urinary: cystoscopy, lithotripsy, urodynamics, renal procedures
16. **"Nervous System, Eye, Ear & Female/Male Genital (60000-69990)"** — Neurostimulator coding, epidural/spinal injections (64400-64530), skull base surgery. Eye: cataract (66982-66984), retinal, glaucoma. Ear: myringotomy, cochlear implant. Female: hysterectomy, laparoscopy, maternity coding. Male: prostatectomy, circumcision
17. **"Radiology, Pathology & Medicine (70000-99199)"** — Radiology: professional vs technical component (-26/-TC), supervision & interpretation, CT/MRI/US, interventional radiology, mammography, radiation oncology. Pathology: clinical vs anatomic, surgical pathology levels (88302-88309). Medicine: immunizations (admin + product), infusions/injections (96360-96379), allergy, neurology, cardiology

### Unit 4: Outpatient Facility Coding & Special Topics (L18-22)
18. **"Outpatient Hospital Coding & APCs"** — Facility vs professional coding, OPPS (Outpatient Prospective Payment System), APCs (Ambulatory Payment Classifications), status indicators (S, T, V, N, Q), packaging, composite APCs, C-APCs (comprehensive APCs), condition code 44, modifier -27 (multiple outpatient encounters), revenue codes
19. **"ASC Coding & Payment"** — Ambulatory Surgery Center payment system, ASC-covered surgical procedures list, ASC payment groups, separately payable drugs/devices, ASC quality reporting, ASC vs HOPD payment comparison
20. **"ICD-10-CM Outpatient Guidelines (Section IV)"** — Outpatient coding guidelines: code to highest level of certainty (NO uncertain diagnoses), first-listed diagnosis vs principal diagnosis, chronic conditions, coexisting conditions, patients receiving diagnostic services, preoperative evaluations, routine prenatal visits, encounters for circumstances other than disease
21. **"Coding for Special Services"** — Telehealth coding (POS 02/10, modifier -95, GT), remote patient monitoring (RPM: 99453-99458), chronic care management (CCM: 99490-99491), behavioral health integration, advance care planning, preventive medicine (99381-99397 vs 99401-99412), Medicare AWV (G0438/G0439)
22. **"Capstone: Integrated Ambulatory Coding Cases"** — Complex multi-code outpatient scenarios, E/M + procedure same day, modifier usage, NCCI edit navigation, real CMS-1500 claim completion, denial resolution, coding career paths (CPC, COC, specialty credentials), AAPC vs AHIMA certification comparison
